NGOs and Students Demand "Highest Level" of Protection for Comino

VALLETTA — A coalition of student organizations and environmental NGOs has voiced strong opposition to the Environment and Resources Authority's (ERA) recent decision regarding Comino.

The group is protesting the approval of excavation works and the uprooting of 678 trees to facilitate a private development project. The permit would allow for the construction of 16 holiday villas, 44 swimming pools, and a 140-bed hotel within the Comino Hotel Complex.
