Yorgen Fenech Trial Faces Potential Dissolution Over Juror Tablet Controversy

VALLETTA — The high-profile trial of businessman Yorgen Fenech has reached a dramatic impasse following revelations regarding the tools used by the jury. Defense lawyers have formally requested that Judge Edwina Grima dissolve the proceedings, alleging that tablets provided to jurors allowed unauthorized access to news headlines and images via Google.

This legal challenge comes on the heels of emotional testimony from Andrew Caruana Galizia, who provided a harrowing account of his mother's final moments. The court must now determine if the potential exposure to media coverage has irrevocably compromised the impartiality of the jury.
