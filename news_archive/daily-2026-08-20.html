Yorgen Fenech Trial: Courtroom Clashes Over Jury Tablets and Social Media

VALLETTA, 20 August 2026 — The Yorgen Fenech murder trial entered a period of intense legal dispute on Wednesday as the defence raised alarms regarding the use of court-issued tablets by jurors.

Defence lawyer Charles Mercieca argued that the devices may have exposed jurors to news headlines, potentially influencing the verdict. Judge Edwina Grima has ordered an IT expert to examine the tablets to determine if any unauthorized internet access occurred during the proceedings.
