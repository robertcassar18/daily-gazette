Kurt Farrugia Appointed to Dual Government Roles Following Transport Malta Sacking

VALLETTA — Former Transport Malta CEO Kurt Farrugia has been handed two significant government appointments, serving as both the chairman of the National Development and Social Fund (NDSF) and the CEO of Residency Malta.

The appointments come just weeks after Farrugia was removed from his position at Transport Malta. The NDSF is responsible for managing funds from the citizenship programme, overseeing projects in education, research, and public health.
