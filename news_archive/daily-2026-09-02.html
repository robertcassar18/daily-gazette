Jury Deliberates in Landmark Daphne Caruana Galizia Murder Trial

VALLETTA — In a moment that has gripped the nation, the jury in the trial of Yorgen Fenech has retired to consider their verdict. The businessman is accused of complicity in the 2017 car bomb murder of prominent anti-corruption journalist Daphne Caruana Galizia.

Judge Edwina Grima concluded her summing up on Wednesday morning, instructing the nine jurors after three days of detailed legal guidance. Fenech faces a potential life sentence if convicted of complicity to murder. Members of the Caruana Galizia family were present in court as the proceedings reached this critical turning point.
