Yorgen Fenech Trial Enters Critical Phase as Defence Challenges Key Witness

VALLETTA, Monday, 17 August 2026 — The trial of Yorgen Fenech, accused of conspiring to murder journalist Daphne Caruana Galizia, entered its 41st day today with intense proceedings in court.

Defence lawyers launched a heavy cross-examination of state witness Melvin Theuma, accusing him of tailoring his evidence to secure a presidential pardon and protect high-ranking officials. The courtroom remained under heavy security as the trial continues to probe the connections between the accused and the 2017 assassination.
