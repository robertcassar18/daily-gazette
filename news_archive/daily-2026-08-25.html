Prosecution Rests Case in Yorgen Fenech Murder Trial

VALLETTA — The prosecution has formally rested its case in the high-profile trial of Yorgen Fenech, accused of complicity in the murder of investigative journalist Daphne Caruana Galizia.

The proceedings reached a significant milestone following the emotional testimony of Andrew Caruana Galizia, the victim's son, who recounted the harrowing moments following the explosion. The defense is now expected to call between four and five witnesses to present their case.
