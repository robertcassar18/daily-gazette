Defence Begins Presentation in High-Profile Yorgen Fenech Trial

VALLETTA — The defence is set to begin presenting its case today in the long-running trial of Yorgen Fenech, marking the 45th day of proceedings.

Following the prosecution's formal conclusion of its case on Thursday evening, legal teams are preparing to challenge the evidence regarding the 2017 assassination of journalist Daphne Caruana Galizia. The court is expected to hear testimony regarding the technical aspects of the crime scene and previous investigations.
