Heated Clash in Yorgen Fenech Trial as Hitman Accuses Middleman of Lying

VALLETTA, Malta — A high-stakes confrontation erupted in the courtroom on Thursday between convicted hitman George Degiorgio and middleman Melvin Theuma during the ongoing trial of Yorgen Fenech.

Degiorgio alleged that Theuma's testimony regarding the involvement of high-ranking officials in the Daphne Caruana Galizia assassination is false. The court has since issued a temporary ban on media reporting the trial proceedings for Friday and Saturday to prevent further interference.
