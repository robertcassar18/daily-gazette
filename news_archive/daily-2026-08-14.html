Court Orders Media Ban Amidst Explosive Testimony in Fenech Trial

A dramatic confrontation erupted in court yesterday between convicted hitman George Degiorgio and self-confessed murder middleman Melvin Theuma, during the ongoing Yorgen Fenech trial by jury. The exchange, which saw the two men trading heated accusations and nearly coming to physical blows, prompted immediate intervention by security officers. Degiorgio reportedly branded Theuma "the biggest liar" during the cross-examination regarding the involvement of high-ranking officials in the plot to murder journalist Daphne Caruana Galizia.

In a significant development, the court has issued a decree banning all media reporting of the trial proceedings for today, Friday, and tomorrow, Saturday. This move follows the intense volatility witnessed during Thursday's session. Legal experts suggest the ban is intended to prevent further prejudice to the jury as the trial enters a critical phase of testimony regarding secret recordings and alleged political connections.
