Court Imposes Reporting Ban on High-Profile Yorgen Fenech Trial

VALLETTA — In a significant development for the ongoing legal proceedings involving Yorgen Fenech, the court has issued a decree banning media from reporting on the jury proceedings for today and tomorrow. The order, which took effect immediately, aims to protect the integrity of the trial as it enters a critical phase with witnesses providing intense testimony.

The decision follows a volatile session in which hitman George Degiorgio and state witness Melvin Theuma traded bitter accusations before the jury — a confrontation that required security intervention and sent shockwaves through the legal community. Media outlets including MaltaToday have confirmed they will pause live coverage in full compliance with the directive.
