Court Imposes Reporting Ban on High-Profile Yorgen Fenech Trial

VALLETTA — In a significant development for the ongoing legal proceedings involving Yorgen Fenech, the court has issued a decree banning media from reporting on the jury proceedings for today and tomorrow. The order, which took effect immediately, aims to protect the integrity of the trial as it enters a critical phase.

The decision follows intense courtroom sessions in which hitman George Degiorgio and state witness Melvin Theuma traded bitter accusations before the jury. Legal experts suggest the reporting ban is intended to prevent prejudice during the jury's deliberations. Media outlets including MaltaToday have confirmed they will pause live coverage in compliance with the directive.
