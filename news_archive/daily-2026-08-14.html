Court Imposes Media Ban on Yorgen Fenech Trial Proceedings

VALLETTA — A court order has been issued banning the media from reporting on the Yorgen Fenech trial for Friday and Saturday.

The decision comes as the jury trial continues, with the court seeking to manage the intense public interest and the sensitive nature of the ongoing testimony. Legal experts suggest the censorship notice aims to protect the integrity of the judicial process during critical witness examinations.
