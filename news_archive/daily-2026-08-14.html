Court Imposes Media Ban Amid Heated Fenech Trial Confrontation

VALLETTA — The high-profile trial of Yorgen Fenech has taken a dramatic turn as the court issued an immediate ban on media reporting of the proceedings for Friday and Saturday. The order comes following a volatile session in which hitman George Degiorgio and middleman Melvin Theuma engaged in a heated exchange, trading accusations in front of the jury.

The confrontation, which required security intervention, centered on the credibility of Theuma, who has been defending his testimony against claims of previous falsehoods. Degiorgio has alleged that high-ranking officials were involved in the plot to kill journalist Daphne Caruana Galizia, a claim that has sent shockwaves through the legal community. Legal experts suggest the reporting ban is intended to protect the integrity of the jury during this critical phase of the compilation of evidence.
