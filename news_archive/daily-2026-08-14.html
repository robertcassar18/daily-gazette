Heated Clash in Fenech Trial: Degiorgio and Theuma Trade Accusations

VALLETTA — The trial by jury of Yorgen Fenech reached a fever pitch on Thursday as a dramatic confrontation erupted between self-confessed murder middleman Melvin Theuma and hitman George Degiorgio. The exchange, which required the intervention of security officers, saw the two men trade heated accusations regarding the involvement of high-ranking officials in the plot to kill journalist Daphne Caruana Galizia.

Theuma, who has faced intense scrutiny over his credibility and his presidential pardon, admitted to "bluffing" in the past but insisted his current testimony is the truth. Degiorgio, however, challenged Theuma's claims, alleging that the middleman was being untruthful about the specifics of the commission.

In a significant development, the court has issued a decree banning all media reporting of the trial proceedings for Friday and Saturday. This move follows concerns regarding the publication of sensitive chats and the potential impact on the integrity of the jury proceedings. Legal experts suggest the ban will remain in place until the court deems it appropriate to lift the restrictions.
