Court Imposes Reporting Ban on High-Profile Yorgen Fenech Trial

VALLETTA — In a significant development for the ongoing legal proceedings involving Yorgen Fenech, the court has issued a decree banning the media from reporting on the jury proceedings for today and tomorrow. The order, which takes effect immediately, aims to protect the integrity of the trial as it continues.

The decision comes as the trial enters a critical phase, with witnesses providing intense testimony regarding the complex web of connections surrounding the case. Legal experts suggest the move is intended to prevent potential prejudice during the jury's deliberations. Media outlets, including MaltaToday, have confirmed they will pause live coverage in compliance with the court's directive.

The trial has already seen dramatic moments, including heated clashes in the courtroom between key figures and testimony regarding alleged hush money and secret communications. The ban is expected to be lifted following the conclusion of the scheduled sessions.

Environmental Wins: Madliena Woodland Protected

The Environment Ministry has announced that the woodland surrounding the historic Fort Madliena has been officially designated as a Tree Protection Area (TPA). The area has also been enhanced with the planting of approximately 1,000 new trees, marking a significant step in local conservation efforts.

White Rocks Designated as National Park

In a move to bolster public spaces, the Cabinet of Ministers has approved the legal notice establishing White Rocks as a National Park. This decision follows long-standing calls for the protection and formal management of the area for public and ecological benefit.
