Yorgen Fenech Faces Intense Cross-Examination in Daphne Caruana Galizia Murder Trial

VALLETTA — The high-profile trial of Yorgen Fenech continues today at the Courts of Justice, with the prosecution intensifying its questioning regarding his alleged complicity in the 2017 assassination of investigative journalist Daphne Caruana Galizia.

The proceedings have drawn significant public attention as the court delves into the complex interface between criminality and politics. Fenech has maintained his innocence, testifying that he had no role in the murder during recent sessions of the trial.
