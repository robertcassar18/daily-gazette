Yorgen Fenech Testifies: "I Never Ordered the Murder"

VALLETTA — In a pivotal moment for the ongoing jury trial, Yorgen Fenech has begun his testimony as the primary defense witness, addressing the 2017 assassination of journalist Daphne Caruana Galizia.

Fenech admitted to passing €50,000 to the hitman, claiming he acted out of "panic" to have the hit called off, but he maintained that he played no role in ordering the assassination. The court is currently reviewing whether the jury's impartiality was compromised by recent news headlines.
