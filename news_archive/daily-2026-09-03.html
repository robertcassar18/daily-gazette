Yorgen Fenech Acquitted in Daphne Caruana Galizia Murder Trial

VALLETTA, Malta — A jury has found prominent Maltese businessman Yorgen Fenech not guilty of complicity and criminal conspiracy in the 2017 car bomb assassination of investigative journalist Daphne Caruana Galizia.

The verdict, delivered after eight hours of deliberation, has sparked intense reactions across the island, with crowds gathering in Valletta to protest the decision. Fenech was the last of the six individuals charged in the high-profile case to be prosecuted.
