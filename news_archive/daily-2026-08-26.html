Yorgen Fenech Trial Reaches Critical Turning Point

VALLETTA — The ongoing trial of Yorgen Fenech, accused of complicity in the assassination of investigative journalist Daphne Caruana Galizia, has entered a high-stakes phase in Courtroom 22.

Defense lawyers have raised significant concerns regarding the use of digital evidence, prompting Judge Edwina Grima to consider a request to discharge the jury. The proceedings remain under intense scrutiny as the court weighs the impact of data extracted from electronic devices on the case.
