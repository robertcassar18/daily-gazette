Farrugia accepts second national appointment amid governance debate

VALLETTA — Former Transport Malta CEO Kurt Farrugia has been named Chairman of the National Development and Social Fund while retaining his current role as CEO of Residency Malta.

The move has prompted discussion in Parliament about role concentration, public oversight, and whether a single executive should guide both investor programmes and national development spending. Analysts say the appointment highlights Malta's reliance on experienced administrators as it prepares high-value infrastructure and education projects.

Officials promise a public briefing this week; opposition leaders call for clearer compensation details and stronger accountability measures.
