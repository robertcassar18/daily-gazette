Yorgen Fenech Acquitted in Daphne Caruana Galizia Murder Trial

VALLETTA, 05 September 2026 — In a verdict that has stunned the nation, a jury has acquitted businessman Yorgen Fenech of all charges related to the 2017 assassination of investigative journalist Daphne Caruana Galizia.

The decision follows months of intense courtroom drama and testimony from over 100 witnesses. The prosecution has already announced its intention to appeal the verdict, seeking a retrial on grounds related to the jury trial process.
