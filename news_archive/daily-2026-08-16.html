Yorgen Fenech Trial: Secret Recordings and Financial Pressure at Center of Testimony

VALLETTA — The high-profile murder trial of Yorgen Fenech has reached a pivotal moment as prosecution witness Melvin Theuma takes the stand.

Theuma, a self-confessed middleman in the 2017 assassination of journalist Daphne Caruana Galizia, testified that secret recordings were his only means of proving his version of events. He detailed the intense financial pressure he faced from the Degiorgio brothers following the murder.
